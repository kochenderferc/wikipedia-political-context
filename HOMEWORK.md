#### Reflection:
Creating the AI workflow for the literature review changed our perspective from manually summarizing papers to synthesizing with ChatGPT as a tool. This also helped us learn about structuring our LLM prompts. However, we did have to manually check summaries and information generated by AI, so it didn't save a huge amount of time.

With LaTeX, we didn't run into a lot of issues. We decided to do our work in Overleaf and just manually copy the files over to GitHub so that we could work collaboratively on the paper more easily. When we used ChatGPT to try generating BibTeX entries, it sometimes messed up on the formatting; Google Scholar's BibTeX entries were reliable.

The next steps for our paper are to flesh out the paper further with our results once we get some feedback and analyze them quantitatively. We will also edit and revise the sections (e.g. abstract, introduction, etc.) that we have already drafted.
